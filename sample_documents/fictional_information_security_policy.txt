FICTIONAL INFORMATION SECURITY POLICY
FOR DEMONSTRATION ONLY

Blue Orchard Studio is an imaginary organization.

Access reviews
System owners review privileged access every 90 days. Dormant accounts are disabled after 45 consecutive days without a successful sign-in.

Incident reporting
Team members report a suspected security incident to the fictional response desk within one hour of discovery. The response lead assigns an initial severity within four hours.

Backups
Critical project repositories are backed up daily. A restore exercise is completed every six months, and results are recorded in the local continuity register.

This sample contains no real security procedures, contacts, or confidential information.
